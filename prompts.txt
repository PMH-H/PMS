You are a senior full-stack performance engineer and healthcare-grade systems architect.

Context:

- React + Vite (PWA)

Problem Statement:
The application experiences slow data population after reload or reopening, even when the app was recently closed. Some dashboards take time to reflect new data, and realtime updates occasionally feel delayed.

Your Mission:
